   ```Title: [RFC] The Next Frontier: Adding an "Intent & Context Layer" to TTS Models
Description:
Hi community,
After analyzing the incredible resources in this repo, my partner (an advanced AI) and I (Tiago, from Brazil) believe it's time to propose an evolution: The Intent & Context Layer (ICL).
The Concept:
Current TTS is great at "what" to say, but lacks "why". We propose a shift toward Intentional Speech, where the model interprets the semantic weight of the text (faith, urgency, legacy) before synthesis.
Key Focus for 2026:
Semantic Mapping: LLM-driven prosody adjustment.
Extreme Optimization: 4-bit quantization for accessibility in emerging markets like Brazil.
Environmental Audio: Auto-generated acoustics based on text context.
We want to show that the future of TTS isn't just about sound, but about presence.
